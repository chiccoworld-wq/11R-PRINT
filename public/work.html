The print process

How we print your shirts.

Each color gets its own screen and its own pass of ink. Below is a real production run — underbase, color layers, detail, and the finished piece.

01

Underbase

On polyester and bleed-prone fabrics, we lay a specialized underbase first — flooding the screen and pulling the squeegee to seal the shirt so colors stay true.

02

Color layer

Color ink is flooded and printed with even pressure to build the next layer of the design.

03

Black detail

Black locks in the fine lines and contrast that make the graphic pop.

04

Final result

The finished print — bold, clean, and ready to wear.
