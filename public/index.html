The craft

Ink through mesh. Not on top of it.

Screen printing forces ink into the garment fibers. You get rich, opaque color, sharp edges, and a finish that survives wash after wash. That's why crews, shops, and brands still choose it.

FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

What's the minimum order quantity?

No strict minimum. Small runs are welcome. Larger quantities bring the per-unit cost down — ask for a quote at any quantity.

How much does screen setup cost?

$20 per color, per order. A 2-color design is $40 setup. Orders of 20+ shirts with 3+ colors get a reduced screen fee of $15 per color.

What file formats do you need?

Vector is best — AI, EPS, or PDF. High-res PNG at 300 dpi also works. Don't have print-ready art? Send what you have and we'll help.

How long does an order take?

Standard turnaround is 7–10 business days after proof approval. Need it faster? Tell us your deadline when you quote.

Why screen printing over heat press?

Richer color, better opacity on darks, and prints that last. Transfers crack. Screen prints don't.
