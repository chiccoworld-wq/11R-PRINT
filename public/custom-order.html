Garment Replacement Policy Garments purchased through 11R Print are covered against production errors caused by our shop. If a shop-supplied garment is damaged or misprinted due to our error, we will replace the garment and reprint it at no additional cost.

Customer-supplied garments are accepted with a $2.00 handling fee per item and are not covered by our garment replacement policy. If a production error occurs on a customer-supplied garment, 11R Print will reprint the replacement garment at no additional printing charge; however, the customer is responsible for supplying or purchasing the replacement garment.

Customer-supplied garments may also react differently to heat, ink, pretreatment, and curing. For this reason, 11R Print cannot guarantee results on garments that were not purchased through our shop.
